The Vision

Empowering the Open Source Ecosystem.

Hacktoberfest Hack Day Bengaluru 2026 is an immersive, in-person hack day run by the AWS Student Builder Group at Atria Institute of Technology[cite: 1]. It unites passionate student builders, developers, and creators to contribute to open-source software, build innovative technical solutions, and foster a collaborative developer community.

The Experience

Challenge Tracks & Building.

Our hack day is structured to push boundaries. We provide the environment, the mentorship, and the sustenance; the hackers provide the ingenuity. We feature 3 challenge tracks[cite: 1], culminating in intensive judging and track recognition announced on stage[cite: 1].

Integration

Platform Adoption

Sponsors have the unique opportunity to challenge hackers to build on top of their proprietary APIs, SDKs, or cloud infrastructure. This drives immediate, hands-on user adoption and generates valuable developer feedback.

Mentorship

Direct Guidance

Send your technical advocates and engineers to mentor teams. This establishes your company as a thought leader and builds authentic relationships with top student developers while they navigate complex technical hurdles.
