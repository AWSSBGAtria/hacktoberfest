Participant Handbook Before you arrive
Step zero

Before you arrive.

This event is open to all university and college students, whether you are a beginner writing your first lines of code or an experienced open-source contributor. There is no registration fee — you get the hack day, workshops, mentor guidance, refreshments and swags for free.

Who can attend

All university and college students. Beginners and experienced contributors are both welcome.

Team size

Solo or in teams of up to 4 members. No team yet? Find one during the morning team formation session.

What it costs

Nothing. No fee, and food and attendee kits are covered while supplies last.

Attendee checklist — tick these off
  • Laptop & Charger (mandatory for hands-on hacking)
  • Valid College / University Student ID Card
  • Active GitHub account (ready to submit PRs)
  • Joined the official WhatsApp group (for live event updates)
  • Git installed on your laptop (we recommend this beforehand)
  • Registration completed on MLH (required for the Attendee Kit)

Built by community

Hacktoberfest is all about celebrating the open source ecosystem. Whether you are submitting your first bug fix or building an entire package, every contribution matters.

Open-weight models

Explore Google Gemma 4 on Ollama, the open SKILL.md agent standard, and Hermes Agent harnesses. Learn to deploy models locally and on AWS cloud services.

Free food, swag & kits

Thanks to our organizers and partners, the entire day is free for university students. Lunch, snacks, stickers and swag — a haul worth over ₹1.4 Lakh across the day.

AWS Student Builder Group

Our student-led user group at Atria Institute of Technology trains students in cloud architecture, serverless systems, and generative AI through hands-on builder hack days.

Challenge tracks

Three ways to build.

Every project can qualify for the core Hacktoberfest challenge, and any project that also uses Gemma 4 or AWS can qualify for those too. One project can take more than one track. Mentors are on-site to help.

Step 01

Pick a track

Core Hacktoberfest, Gemma 4, or AWS. One project can qualify for more than one.

Step 02

Build all day

Mentors from AWS Student Builder Group and open source roam the room from 10:15 AM.

Step 03

Demo and submit

Push your repo, open your PR, and get your project on stage.

Core Hacktoberfest Challenge

Best Open-Source AI Project

The main Hacktoberfest track, run at every Hack Day. Build an original project where open-source or open-weight AI does real work: an agent skill, an open-weight model, or a model harness. Ship it to a public GitHub repo under an open-source license.

The winning team is announced on stage; prizes are confirmed closer to the day.

Google Partner Challenge

Best Use of Gemma 4

Build with Gemma 4, Google's open-weight multimodal model, through the Gemini API. Use it to understand text and images together, or move quickly from an idea to a working prototype.

The winning team is announced on stage; prizes are confirmed closer to the day.

AWS Student Builder Group Special

Best Use of AWS

Our own bonus track for this Hack Day. Build or deploy your project on AWS — Lambda, S3, Bedrock, API Gateway; the strongest AWS-powered entry takes the track, on top of any Hacktoberfest challenge it also qualifies for.

Q1Who can attend this Hack Day?

This event is open to all university and college students, whether you are a beginner writing your first lines of code or an experienced open-source contributor.

Q2Is there any registration fee?

No! The event is completely free of charge. You get access to the hack day, workshops, mentor guidance, refreshments/food, and swags for free.

Q3What should I bring to the event?

Bring your laptop, charger, student ID card, and have a valid GitHub account. We recommend having Git installed on your laptop beforehand.

Q4Can I participate individually or do I need a team?

You can participate solo or in teams of up to 4 members. If you don't have a team yet, you can easily find teammates during the morning team formation session.

Q5I am a complete beginner to Open Source. Can I still join?

Absolutely! Hacktoberfest is built to be welcoming to all levels. We will have dedicated mentors to guide you through Git commands, finding good first issues, and submitting clean pull requests.

Q6How does the MLH registration work?

Click any 'Register Now' button on the site to go to the official MLH event page. Log in or create an MLH account, answer the questions (like your college name & phone number), and join the official WhatsApp group for live announcements.
